# Power BI Dashboard Layout

**Page 1 — Executive Overview:** KPI cards, recovery by portfolio, aging exposure and collector leaderboard.

**Page 2 — Collector Performance:** collected dollars, recovery %, RPC rate, dollars/RPC and settlement rate.

**Page 3 — Risk & Aging:** 90+ DPD exposure, default rate, credit-score bands and high-risk worklist.

**Page 4 — Account Drillthrough:** balance, DPD, contacts, RPC, PTP, settlement and risk fields.
